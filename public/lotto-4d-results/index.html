How Lotto 4D Works

Lotto 4D is a popular numbers game in Malaysia where players pick a four-digit number (from 0000 to 9999) and place a bet on it. If the number you choose matches the winning combination drawn by the provider (like Magnum, Toto, or Damacai), you win a prize based on your bet type and amount. There are several betting methods, including Big, Small, iBox, and others, which determine your payout odds and win criteria.

Typically, draws are held multiple times a week, and each draw features a set of winning numbers categorized into 1st Prize, 2nd Prize, 3rd Prize, Special Prizes, and Consolation Prizes.
